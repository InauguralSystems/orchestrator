## <Specialist title> — `<skill-name>`
- **Mission**: the one axis of work this employee owns (e.g. "the runtime and
  its performance", "the release pipeline and cross-platform builds").
- **Owns**: the specific repos / directories / surfaces this role is
  accountable for.
- **How the job is done**: the method — the concrete steps, tools, and review
  loop this role follows. Point at the handbook skills it inherits
  (`workflow`, plus any shared training).
- **Pass** (the mechanical bar): a single checkable condition that means the
  work is good — a passing gate command, a verified measurement, a merged
  artifact. Not a vibe.
- **Fail**: the shapes of a bad deliverable for this role (e.g. an unverified
  claim, a workaround where an upstream fix was possible, a red gate).
